# Commercial migration program

## Entry condition

Begin only from an accepted personal release and its final handoff. This program converts that release into a multi-user commercial service, migrates application compute and PostgreSQL from Coolify to Render Frankfurt, and closes the architecture's deferred commercial launch gates. Cloudflare R2, Auth0, provider-neutral AI/speech ports, and app-owned identifiers remain portable unless evidence requires a compatible migration.

Do not decommission Coolify during preparation or rehearsal. Do not claim legal, content-rights, privacy, trademark, fairness, penetration-test, or accessibility sign-off without qualified evidence.

## Sequential stages

1. **Baseline audit and closure register** — verify the personal release against architecture, inventory debt and data, resolve authority conflicts, and create a complete commercial closure/cutover register.
2. **Multi-user product, identity, authorization, and abuse controls** — restore account-optional/guest adoption or explicitly supersede it, implement tenant-safe ownership, production Auth0/account lifecycle, quotas, support boundaries, and abuse controls.
3. **Render platform, environments, CI/CD, and operations** — produce owner guides and infrastructure for Render Frankfurt web/worker/PostgreSQL, staging/production, domains, secrets, observability, backups, rollbacks, and controlled delivery while Coolify remains live.
4. **Data, object, job, and identity migration rehearsals** — rehearse compatible PostgreSQL and supporting-state migration, validate counts/checksums/version pins/deletion journals, test rollback, and publish a timed runbook.
5. **Commercial evidence and deferred-item closure** — close content rights, privacy/security, accessibility, evaluator/fairness, reliability/performance/cost, processor, support, pricing, and documentation gates with honest evidence.
6. **Production cutover and final commercial validation** — execute the approved low-downtime migration, switch ingress/domain and Auth0 callbacks, validate end to end, observe, retain a bounded rollback window, and publish the final commercial handoff.

For each number, run `NNP` to acceptance before `NNE`. Do not run stages concurrently.

## Completion definition

The commercial program is complete only after all six Execution rows are independently verified; qualified external gates are accepted; multi-user isolation and account lifecycle are proven; Render is the reproducible production platform; rollback and recovery are rehearsed; Coolify disposition is explicitly authorized; and the final release manifest identifies the exact code, schema, content, policy, model/prompt, Design, and infrastructure versions.

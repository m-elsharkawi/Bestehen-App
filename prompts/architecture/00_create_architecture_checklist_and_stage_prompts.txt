## 3. Product and Design Invariants Every Architecture Stage Must Respect

The checklist and prompts must embed these invariants wherever relevant. They are not decorative UI rules.

### 3.1 Product boundary

- Bestehen / App is the learner product; Bestehen / Corpus supplies versioned intelligence and rights-aware metadata.
- Normal learner attempts must not require live Corpus search or per-click Corpus AI analysis.
- Raw/reference source access is not a learner runtime dependency.
- Corpus frequency/relevance are priors and content intelligence, never learner mastery or weakness.
- Authenticity/confidence and usage rights are separate.

### 3.2 Evidence truth

- Learning evidence and Readiness evidence remain distinct.
- Direct modality evidence cannot be replaced by cross-skill inference.
- Assisted, open-timed, exposed, retried, pending, interrupted, invalid, and target-condition observations remain distinguishable.
- A technical, content, media, accessibility, or evaluator failure never becomes learner failure.
- Immediate retry responsiveness is not delayed retention or resolved mastery.
- Fresh-content family independence matters for transfer, mocks, and booking confirmation.
- Historical evidence remains auditable when current freshness or validity changes.
- All calculations that affect learner claims must be versioned and explainable.

The design currently names five learner-visible evidence-validity states:

`valid` · `partially_valid` · `pending` · `interrupted` · `not_counted`

Architecture must preserve their distinct semantics and affected scope. Do not collapse them into a success/error boolean.

### 3.3 Readiness and exam truth

- Written and Oral are independent aggregates and pass gates.
- An overall total cannot compensate for failure in either component.
- No pass-probability field, calculation, API, event, template, or hidden model is allowed before the empirically calibrated product gate permits it.
- `evidence_building` means absence of a numeric estimate, not zero.
- Readiness bands and reasons are discriminated states, not a color or a boolean.
- Safe and Ready to Book are different: Ready to Book is a gate over current Readiness/evidence, not a higher score.
- The official pass line and Bestehen's safety margin must remain distinguishable and versioned.
- A blueprint change triggers targeted revalidation/recalculation rather than silent reinterpretation.

The current design represents six Readiness states:

`evidence_building` · `not_ready` · `borderline` · `safe` · `ready_to_book` · `stale_contradicted`

The final architecture must explain how each is computed, stored or derived, invalidated, audited, and returned to clients.

### 3.4 Coaching and learner control

- Coach/Home supplies one dominant recommended action.
- `Coach me` and `Just practice X` are distinct paths.
- Learners control time, context, skill choice, ordinary-practice help, postponement, stopping, early mock/target-condition choice, schedule, notification preferences, and explanation language.
- Learners cannot edit target rules, scoring, validity, assistance/timing labels, evidence strength, Readiness interpretation, mistake history, or mock validity.
- One material consequence may be explained factually; no compliance score or punitive behavior exists.

### 3.5 Shared learning loop and skill architecture

- The canonical loop is Observe → Attempt → Diagnose → Repair → Retry → Remember.
- Attempt, evidence, diagnosis, repair exposure, fresh retry, and delayed retention have different state/evidence meanings.
- Five skills share one attempt/session/result/repair architecture; skill-specific input regions extend it.
- Do not fork five unrelated feature stacks that drift in evidence, timing, accessibility, or error behavior.
- P0–P4 are condition profiles by task family/skill lane, never one global course level.

### 3.6 Content, media, and productive evaluation

- Reference intelligence, reusable source assets, curated gold content, validated original practice, and adaptive/ephemeral interaction remain different content classes.
- Rights and attribution travel with exact reusable assets.
- Objective scoring is deterministic by default.
- Routine content is pre-generated/authored, reviewed, cached, and reused; runtime generation is exceptional.
- Writing/Speaking evaluation is structured, versioned, confidence-aware, bounded, and may become Pending.
- One justified premium escalation is the default ceiling; persistent uncertainty stays visible.
- Raw voice is ephemeral by default and deleted after finalized evaluation, with the current pending ceiling preserved.

### 3.7 Platform, accessibility, recovery, and data

- Launch is responsive web, phone-first for daily coaching, with stronger large-screen layouts for Writing, Readiness, and mocks.
- Browser/device capability degrades locally; one missing capability must not block the whole App.
- WCAG 2.2 AA core flows, keyboard access, screen-reader semantics, 200% zoom/reflow, reduced motion, non-color-only meaning, text alternatives, and drag-free alternatives are architecture acceptance criteria.
- Timer ticks are not announced; live-region priority and focus movement must follow the design contract.
- Drafts/checkpoints, timers, media, interruption, resume, and idempotency must preserve actual conditions and evidence scope.
- Core first use is account-optional. Future sync/recovery may justify identity.
- Learner data remains exportable/deletable; AI receives the minimum necessary data.

### 3.8 Cost and operational sustainability

- This is a personal/private project initially.
- Prefer the smallest maintainable architecture that preserves the complete exam-coaching goal.
- Deterministic logic, caching, batching, precomputation, and approved reusable assets are the default.
- Do not introduce microservices, Kubernetes, event-streaming platforms, multi-region deployment, heavy data warehousing, always-on premium AI, or enterprise abstractions without a demonstrated requirement.
- Managed services may be appropriate when they materially reduce solo maintenance, but their recurring and exit costs must be explicit.
- Cost chooses among equivalent-quality options; it cannot remove pass-critical Writing/Oral evidence merely because objective work is cheaper.

## 8. Architecture Decision Record Strategy

The planning run must define a lightweight ADR approach suitable for a personal project.

Recommended default:

- ADRs live under `Architecture/adr/`;
- filenames use `ADR-0001-short-title.md`;
- stages create ADRs only for consequential, durable choices with meaningful alternatives;
- stage documents remain the full rationale/handoff; ADRs are concise indexes of final decisions;
- superseded decisions are marked rather than deleted;
- the final architecture specification includes an ADR index and status.

The master planning run should not create speculative ADRs for technology choices that later stages have not evaluated. It should create the ADR template and rules only if useful, or assign that creation explicitly to Stage 01.

## 9. Cost-Control Requirements for the Prompt Pack

Every relevant stage prompt must force explicit cost reasoning.

The architecture must favor:

- deterministic scoring, Readiness, review, and plan logic;
- cached/pre-generated routine content;
- asynchronous Corpus intelligence publication;
- bounded Writing/Speaking AI evaluation;
- batched/cached TTS and content generation;
- simple managed infrastructure where it saves meaningful solo-maintenance time;
- progressive capacity upgrades based on measured usage;
- one source of truth rather than duplicated systems;
- transparent per-capability cost measurement.

It must reject by default:

- per-click premium AI;
- runtime generation for routine objective questions;
- always-on AI summaries/analytics;
- expensive real-time voice when turn-based interaction satisfies the product;
- infrastructure designed for hypothetical millions of users;
- independent services for every domain concept;
- an analytics warehouse before the lean event set proves insufficient;
- provider lock-in without export/version/fallback strategy.

Stage prompts selecting mutable commercial services must require current official pricing/quota research and a cheaper fallback. Do not invent a fixed euro budget if the owner has not supplied one; define cost drivers, measurement, alert thresholds, and upgrade triggers.

## 10. Security, Privacy, Rights, and Safety Requirements

Prompts must treat these as architecture, not legal boilerplate:

- data minimization and purpose limitation;
- account-optional first use;
- pseudonymous external AI calls;
- raw-audio ephemerality and pending ceiling;
- Writing/transcript/evidence retention and deletion;
- learner export and full deletion orchestration;
- rights-aware content serving and no unauthorized source redistribution;
- secrets and provider credential boundaries;
- authentication and authorization only where necessary;
- abuse/rate limiting proportionate to private/public scope;
- auditability for evidence invalidation, blueprint/evaluator changes, and deletion;
- no hidden model-training contribution without explicit opt-in;
- no invasive session replay requirement;
- no claim of legal compliance without later jurisdiction-specific review.

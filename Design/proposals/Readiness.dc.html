WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY EACH ONE EARNS ITS PLACE
a
Two reference lines, told apart. Solid dark is TELC's 60. Dashed green is ours at 65, labelled as ours on the chart itself — so the safety margin can never be mistaken for the exam's rule. Bklit's Reference Line, doing the job Handoff §7.1 asks for.
b
The range is a measured area, not a texture. Bordered on both edges, numbered inside, on a 10% gridded track. “Span 13” in the header states the uncertainty as a quantity — the thing confidence actually means.
c
The audit trail shares the axis. The desktop doc puts the evidence list in a side panel; here each attempt sits at its own score, under the estimate it produced, with condition and age. The claim and its inputs read as one graphic. Mobile keeps the same rows, stacked.
d
Absence is drawn. S3 gets a dashed rule across the full width with the words NEVER ATTEMPTED — the P4 rule (“missing evidence gets a designed slot”) applied inside the chart rather than as a grey table row.
e
Breadth as a ring, in neutral grey. Bklit's Ring Chart, deliberately drawn in the system family, not green — it is a completeness meter, not an achievement. It answers the question the number cannot: why a good score still isn't safe.
f
The conclusion sits on ink. The bottleneck sentence and its one action are the only elements on the card that are not on a white surface. That is P2 arriving early — see the next artboard.
THE ONE I AM RECOMMENDING AGAINST
Bklit — Projection Line
Readiness over time with a dashed extrapolation reaching the exam date. It is the most attractive chart in the reference set, it would look excellent here, and I think it should not be built.
PASS 60
EXAM · 14.11
A dashed line that crosses the pass line before the exam date is a pass prediction with the number filed off. The prohibition in Spec 28 is not about the word “probability” — it is about the claim. A learner who sees this will book the exam because of it, and the design will have made a promise the model is not entitled to make.
Instead: the breadth ring and the evidence-age column already answer “am I moving?” honestly, backwards, from things that happened. If a trend is wanted, draw the past attempts and stop at today — which is exactly what the attempt rows do.
ACCESSIBILITY · UNCHANGED OBLIGATIONS
The chart is richer, so the sentence before it and the table behind Show evidence as text both grow. Every dot needs a row: score, subsection, condition label, age, validity. The role="img" label becomes two sentences — the estimate and its range, then the breadth gap. Colour still carries nothing alone: exam-versus-practice is filled-versus-hollow first, and every dot has a word in the table.

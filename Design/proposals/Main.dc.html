BESTEHEN / APP·DESIGN REVIEW·PROPOSALS, NOT YET APPLIED

The thinking is finished. The drawing barely started.

Four documents, 41 components, 24 screens, a twelve-case failure table and an accessibility contract written as test cases. The system is better than most shipped products ever get.

The rendering of it is one rectangle, repeated. There is no focal point on any screen, and the element Identity calls the visual centre of the brand is a 28-pixel striped bar in the corner of a card.

SIX OBSERVATIONS
01
Every surface weighs the same.
A coach's diagnosis, a settings row, a task item and a footnote are all a white box with a hairline. Elevation is scarce by design — correct — but scarcity was applied to contrast too, and contrast is free.
01 §2 · 02 ALL ACTS
02
Readiness is drawn as a progress bar it explicitly must not be.
The model already carries range_low/high, confidence, evidence_age and breadth_complete. The bar renders two of six. A striped fill cannot say “wide range, thin evidence, three weeks old.”
01 §3.3 · 03 D2
03
Bricolage is specified, then never allowed to speak.
Inside the phone frames the display face appears once per screen at 26–27px and then hands everything to Plex Sans at 15px. The ramp is 27 → 16 → 15 → 13. That is four sizes doing one job; a screen with no size contrast has no reading order.
01 §1.4 · 02 ACT 2
04
The coach's voice has no visual form.
Coach messages — the product's whole personality — are a white card with a 3px navy stripe on the left. The most distinctive thing Bestehen says is rendered in its least distinctive container, thirteen times.
02 · 1.4, 2.1, 2.3 · 03 D2
05
Motion is specified in prose and drawn nowhere.
120 / 200 / 260 / 600ms are written down with a reduced-motion contract, but not one artboard shows a before and after state. An engineer implementing the 600ms Readiness re-draw has a duration and no picture of what moves.
04 §a · MOTION
06
Device chrome that will be drawn twice.
33 painted status bars, plus a fake browser dot-strip on every desktop frame. Harmless in a spec, but they train the eye to read the mock as finished and hide the real question: what does the top 44px actually contain?

THE THREE REFERENCES

Two of these libraries are useful here. One is mostly a list of things Bestehen has already banned.

Worth saying plainly before we borrow anything: Magic UI is a marketing-page motion library. Confetti, meteors, sparkles, shimmer, rainbow buttons and aurora text are its centre of gravity, and Identity §10 and the hard-prohibition list rule out every one of them by name. It still contributes two components. Bklit contributes the most, because Readiness is a data-visualisation problem the current design solves with a rectangle.

BklitHIGH VALUE
17 chart types on shadcn. The primitives matter more than the charts.
TAKE
Reference line Reference area Ring chart Custom indicator Scatter Brush
LEAVE
Candlestick, Sankey, Sunburst, Choropleth, Funnel, Gauge-as-dial. Nothing in an exam coach is a flow, a hierarchy or a map — and a dial reads as a score.
Also: Projection Line. Genuinely useful, genuinely dangerous — see P1.
KokonutUISELECTIVE
Tailwind v4 + shadcn + Lucide. Same icon family Bestehen already subsets.
TAKE
The composition habits, not the components: dense list rows with a leading glyph and a trailing meta column; toolbars that group by rule instead of by box; cards that carry a distinct header band rather than uniform padding.
LEAVE
Particle Button and the rest of the flourish tier. A button that emits particles when a learner submits a wrong answer is the failure mode Identity §10 is written against.
Magic UIMOSTLY OUT
Special effects, text animations, backgrounds, device mocks.
TAKE · TWO
Number Ticker — the 600ms Readiness re-draw already in 04 §a, finally with an implementation.
Device mocks — as a build reference for the frames, minus the painted status bar.

FOUR PROPOSALS · ON THE ARTBOARDS TO THE RIGHT

Each one is drawn before-and-after, with what it costs.

P1
Readiness becomes an evidence chart.
All six model fields rendered: estimate, range as a reference area, the official 60 line and our 65 margin as two distinguishable reference lines, evidence age, breadth as a coverage ring, and the attempts themselves as conditioned dots. Plus the case against drawing a projection.
COST
New chart primitives. The text alternative table grows. Biggest change in the set.
P2
A real surface ladder, and a voice for the coach.
Four ranks instead of one: ink for what the coach concluded, raised for what you act on, paper for context, sunken for what the system owns. The left-border stripe retires. Coach Home redrawn twice so you can compare a loud reading and a quiet one.
COST
Touches every screen in 02 and 03. No new tokens.
P3
Nine components the system is missing.
Drawn, not tabulated: evidence dot, condition legend, coverage ring, pass-line scale, active plan card, attempt row, band-empty slot, ticker number, conditions disclosure. Six are compositions of tokens you already have.
COST
41 components becomes 50. Three need real engineering.
P4
Motion drawn as frames, not durations.
The four transitions the contract names, each as start → end with its reduced-motion fallback beside it. Includes the one Magic UI import that survives, and the one that looked tempting and does not.
COST
Documentation only. Nothing in the product changes.
What is deliberately not proposed. No new palette, no new type pairing, no new radii, no third theme. The tokens, the fonts, the icon rule and the 4-base spacing scale are right and stay exactly as they are — every proposal below is built from them. Nothing here touches a product rule from Spec 28, and no proposal adds a screen, a destination, or a number the model does not already hold.

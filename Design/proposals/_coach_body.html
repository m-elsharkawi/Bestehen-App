P2 · SURFACE LADDER AND COACH VOICE · AFFECTS EVERY SCREEN IN 02 AND 03

One rectangle, repeated, is why no screen has anywhere to look first.

The fix is not more decoration — it is a rank. Five surface levels already exist in the token set and are used almost interchangeably. Assign them meaning, allow exactly one top-rank block per screen, and the focal point becomes structural instead of decorative.

RANK 4 · INK What the coach concluded
Max one per screen. The diagnosis, the bottleneck, the verdict. If a screen has two, it has no conclusion.
RANK 3 · ACTIVE What you act on now
Raised + border-strong + the e1 shadow the system already reserves for exactly this.
RANK 2 · OBJECT Things in a list
Today the default for everything. Demoted to what it is: a repeated object among peers.
RANK 1 · PAPER Structure and context
Hairline rules and mono labels instead of boxes. Roughly a third of today's 146 cards are groupings, not objects.
RANK 0 · SUNKEN What the system owns
Conditions, validity, evaluator state, “this wasn't you”. Recessed because it is stated, not offered. Matches 01 §3.5.
The rule that does the work: a screen may contain one Rank 4 block and one Rank 3 card. That single constraint gives every screen in the product a guaranteed reading order without adding a token, a colour or a shadow — and it makes “one decision per screen” (P1 in 01 §0) visible instead of merely intended.

RANK 4 · ONCE PER SCREEN
The stripe was carrying two jobs — “this is the coach talking” and “this is important” — with one 3px rule, in a pattern that reads as generated. Splitting them fixes both: the coach gets a surface, importance gets a rank. On the eleven screens where the stripe marked something that was not a conclusion, it simply goes, and the content becomes Rank 1 or Rank 0 text.
MY RECOMMENDATION
Option A for Coach Home, Readiness, Diagnose and Mock Result. Option B everywhere else.
Those four screens are the ones that exist to deliver a verdict, and they are the four where a learner most needs to be told where to look. The other twenty are tasks, forms and lists — they have a natural focus already (the task itself), and an ink block there would compete with it. This also keeps Rank 4 rare enough to mean something: see it, and the coach has concluded something.
Contrast checked, not assumed. Both options use existing token pairs only — on-ink and on-ink-3 on ink, accent-on-ink for the link. The one new pairing is the amber dot on ink, which is graphic-only and always paired with the words Oral −12. In dark mode the ink block goes near-black and stays a different place, exactly as 01 §1.2 requires.

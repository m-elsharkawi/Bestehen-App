00 — Cover · source hierarchy · design principles

The design system for a coach that has to stay honest.

Bestehen / App turns roughly-A1 German into a defensible TELC B1 pass decision. Every surface in this system answers three questions — Where am I? What is holding me back? What should I do next? — and never buys clarity by overstating evidence.

PRIMARY NAV

Design owns visual, interaction, layout, component and responsive decisions. It does not own Readiness semantics, evidence rules, learner-control boundaries, exam conditions, scope, or the coach identity — those are quoted, not reinterpreted.
HARD PROHIBITIONS IN THIS SYSTEM

Pass probability · “62% chance of passing” · mascot or named character · confetti, gems, hearts, lives, currency, leaderboard, dominant streak · AI-chat as the product · TELC logos or accreditation styling · a Corpus browser or provenance dashboard · shame, “we miss you”, arithmetic-only countdowns · coach-control percentage or obedience score · billing, social, enterprise admin.

SIX DESIGN PRINCIPLES
P1

One decision per screen.

Every screen has exactly one dominant action and one supporting reason. Analytics are available by disclosure, never by default.

P2

Evidence before encouragement.

Praise is a consequence of an observation, so it is always attached to the observation that earned it. No unearned warmth.

P3

Two gates, always visible.

Written and Oral never merge into one bar. The overall picture is the weaker of the two, stated as a bottleneck, never as a probability.

P4

Uncertainty is content.

Missing evidence gets a designed slot with a next step, not a greyed number or a spinner. Withhold precision rather than invent it.

P5

Failure has a named owner.

Technical, content and evaluator faults use the neutral system treatment — a different colour family, a different icon, and copy that starts with “This wasn't you.”

P6

Quiet surfaces, loud only when pass-critical.

Cool porcelain or night slate, one accent, thin rules. Red appears only when a component is below the pass line or an answer is wrong. Never two alarms at once.

01 — Foundations and tokens

Tokens named for meaning, not for hue.

The Identity palette is preserved as the brand layer. Semantic tokens sit on top of it, and only semantic tokens are used in components — so “risk” can be re-tuned for contrast without a redesign. Status is never carried by colour alone: every status token pairs with a word, a shape and a fixed position.

RULES
Three roles, three faces. Bricolage Grotesque sets screen titles and section headings only — its irregular terminals give the product a voice at large sizes. IBM Plex Sans carries everything readable, including all numbers. IBM Plex Mono holds labels, timers and evidence metadata. Plex Sans and Plex Mono share a skeleton, so a mono label beside sans data never looks pasted in.
German first. Task content is set in German at body-lg with a 1.7 line height; compound words use hyphens:auto with lang="de", never overflow-wrap:anywhere.
No truncation on meaning. Weakness names, content points and coach reasons wrap. Truncation is allowed only on history rows, where the full string stays available on the detail screen.
Weights. 400 body, 500 emphasis inside data, 600 headings and buttons. 700 is reserved for Bricolage display only.
Display discipline. Bricolage never goes below 22px, never sets a paragraph, never sets a number, and never appears twice on one screen. Below 22px it hands over to Plex Sans 600 — that is the whole rule.

Licensing. All three are SIL OFL, self-hostable at zero cost; fallback stack is Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.

THE RULE
An icon must depict the noun or the verb it stands for. A square, a diamond, a triangle or a bar is not an icon — it is a bullet, and it teaches nothing. If a concept has no honest picture, it gets a word instead. Every glyph above is drawn from the Lucide set (ISC licence, self-hosted subset), 1.75px stroke on a 24 grid, so a new concept can be added without a new drawing style.
HOW THEY ARE USED
Sizes. 15px beside a chip label · 20px inline in text · 24px navigation and section headers · 44px minimum touch target regardless of glyph size.
Never alone. Status icons always sit beside their word, so the meaning survives greyscale, colour-blindness and screen readers; the SVG itself is aria-hidden.
One icon per meaning. No synonyms — Listening is headphones everywhere, and a transcript borrows Reading rather than inventing a glyph.
Nothing celebratory. No trophies, medals, stars, flames, sparkles or characters. Progress is a rising line, not a reward.

1.12 READINESS VISUALISATION GRAMMAR
ANATOMY · component scale, 0–100% of component maximum
Writtenest. 64% · range 60–68
0↑ pass line 60100
Pass line — ink rule, taller than the track, labelled with the number. Always drawn even when the estimate is withheld.
Estimate — single solid marker, not a filled bar, because a fill reads as accumulated points.
Planning range — hatched band. Hatching, not tint, so it survives greyscale and colour-blind vision.
Insufficient evidence — dashed empty track, no marker, no number. Never a 0 and never a grey fill.
MANDATORY TEXT ALTERNATIVE

Every scale ships with a sentence in the DOM before the graphic and a table behind Show evidence as text:

“Written: estimated 64% of 225 points, planning range 60 to 68, pass line 60. Above the pass line, but the range still touches it.”
Charts are role="img" with that sentence as the label; the table is a real table with one row per section. No tooltips-only data, no hover-only labels, no gradients, no doughnuts, no gauge needles.

In-product use is deliberately small. The mark appears at 24px in the mobile utility header, in the desktop sidebar, on the landing screen, and on the focused attempt shell where it is the only branding present. 02 — Components and variants

One library, shared by five skills.

Skills differ in their input mechanism, not in their chrome. Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking all use the same attempt shell, the same result card, the same repair card and the same evidence labels — which is what keeps a five-skill product learnable and stops a second design system from appearing per skill.

Rules. Labels are read-only — a learner can never edit an evidence label, and no override changes one. They sit in a fixed row directly under the task title in an attempt, and directly under the score in a result. Maximum three labels visible; the rest live behind Conditions.

03 — Product patterns and state models

The models a screen has to obey.

Chips in this section carry their word only; the matching icon from §1.9 sits beside it in the product. These are the state machines behind the screens in 02 and 03. Each one names the states, the visual treatment, and the copy rule — so a later screen that isn't drawn here still has one correct answer.

3.1 THE CANONICAL LOOP AS A LAYOUT CONTRACT
1 OBSERVE
Goal, conditions, allowed help, why it matters. Full-width card, one primary Start.
2 ATTEMPT
Focus shell: nav hidden, one task, labels fixed at top, sticky submit.
3 DIAGNOSE
Outcome → decisive evidence → cause + confidence. Never a bare score.
4 REPAIR
One card, one pattern, ≤5 minutes, skippable without penalty.
5 RETRY
Fresh-check banner, same shell as Attempt, no help available.
6 REMEMBER
What changed, what returns and when. Ends the session, no completion percentage.
Conditional steps. A valid correct attempt jumps 2 → 6. A pending evaluator holds 3 and shows the pending banner. A mock suppresses 3–5 entirely until the measured block ends.
Skips are neutral. Skipping repair or retry records the condition and returns the obligation later. There is no “incomplete loop” treatment and no completion score.
Fresh content missing. If no unseen equivalent exists, Retry is replaced by “Transfer check pending — we don't have a fresh task yet.” That is a product gap, stated as ours.
Same shell, five skills. Only the input region changes: choices, cloze, player, editor, or microphone.

3.5 CONTROL BOUNDARY · what a learner can change
LEARNER-CONTROLLED · plain controls, no warnings

Session length · time of day and days per week · which skill · easier or harder · request help in ordinary practice · postpone · stop mid-task · start a mock early · request exam conditions early · notification preferences · explanation language.

A material consequence is stated once, factually, in one sentence: “Help stays available here, so this attempt won't count toward your Reading Readiness.” Then the learner proceeds. No confirmation dialog, no second warning, no tone change.
SYSTEM-OWNED · rendered as text, never as a control

Target blueprint and pass rules · scoring · evidence validity and strength · assistance and timing labels · Readiness interpretation and bands · mistake history · mock validity · what counts as fresh.

These appear as statements with an optional Why? disclosure — never as a field, slider, toggle or advanced setting. There is no coach-control percentage and no compliance score anywhere in the system.

3.7 THE FOUR SENTENCES EVERY SCREEN CAN ANSWER
Where am I?
Band + component state, or an honest “not enough evidence yet”.
What is holding me back?
Exactly one named blocker or gap, with its component.
What should I do next?
One dominant action with a duration.
Who owns this outcome?
The learner, the evaluator, or us — never ambiguous.

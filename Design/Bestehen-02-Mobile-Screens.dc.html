02 — Mobile screen system · 390 × 844 · 24 screens

The whole product, in the order a learner meets it.

Mobile is the primary surface, so it is designed first and completely — including the screens nobody demos: a denied microphone, an interrupted mock, a six-week absence, a deletion. Each frame is annotated with the decision it encodes and the state model from 01 §3 it obeys.

Device corners are 22px because they are a bezel, not a UI surface — inside the frame nothing exceeds the 12px sheet radius. Every primary action clears 48px; the sticky action area reserves 84px plus the safe-area inset.

ACT 1 · ARRIVAL AND PLACEMENT

Earn the first attempt before asking for a commitment.

No account wall, no ten-question survey, no promise of a score. The first session produces one honest observation and one visible consequence — which is the only thing that makes the second session likely.

ACT 3 · THE OTHER FOUR SKILLS · same shell, different input region

Listening, Writing and Speaking change the input — nothing else.

Timer position, conditions row, sticky action and result grammar are identical to Reading. That is what keeps a five-skill product learnable, and it is why Speaking — the hardest to build — feels like the rest of the app rather than a separate product.

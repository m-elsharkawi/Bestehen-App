THE FOUR THINGS THAT WOULD MAKE THIS PRODUCT INACCESSIBLE — AND ARE THEREFORE FORBIDDEN
Colour-only status. Every band, label and answer state carries a word and a named icon as well as its hue.
Chart-only evidence. Every scale has a sentence in the DOM before it and a real table behind one control.
Drag-only interaction. Matching and ordering always have a select-then-place keyboard path producing identical evidence.
Time pressure without control. Timers can be hidden, practice timing can be extended, and only mocks enforce exam timing.

EXPLICITLY NOT DESIGNED HERE
Corpus authoring and provenance tooling · evaluator dashboards · billing and subscription surfaces · account recovery · marketing site · notification copy beyond the two examples shown · any B2 or Goethe variant. Each is out of scope in the specification, and drawing them would imply a commitment the product has not made.
HOW TO REVIEW THIS SYSTEM IN TEN MINUTES
1 · Read three screens. 02 · 2.1, 2.3 and 2.6 — the plan, the diagnosis and the retention screen. If those three are right, the loop is right.
2 · Check the honesty cases. 02 · Act 5 and the evidence-building band in 1.4. This is where a coaching product usually starts lying.
3 · Test the vocabulary. 01 · §2.5 evidence labels. If a label here is wrong, it is wrong in fifty places downstream.
4 · Answer the six open decisions. They are the only things blocking a build-ready spec.

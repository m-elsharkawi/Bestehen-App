U1 — Shell anatomy · two shells, five states

A shell that shows no learner state cannot show navigation.

The authenticated shell is unchanged — 01 · §2.13 and 03 · D1 own it. The delta is a second shell for the moments when there is no session to navigate: it keeps the mark, the type, the tokens and the theme, and it drops everything that implies a place in the product. Four destinations still exist in exactly one shell, so no fifth destination and no per-device IA can appear here.

Shell rules. Shell B never renders a password, e-mail or code field — credential entry belongs to Auth0 Universal Login and appears at its own origin. U2 — SessionNotice · one component, five states

Identity loss is a system event, not a learner mistake.

One component, because all five states have one job: say what happened, say what is safe, offer one way forward. It uses the neutral system family from 01 · §3.6 — grey on mist, dashed edge — so nothing here can be mistaken for a score, a weakness or a red alarm. Cards are shown at 390 px, the primary phone width.

CONTRACT
Validity is untouched. The attempt stays in progress. It does not become interrupted, not_counted or a low result — a session boundary is not an interruption of the task.
Timing survives. Elapsed time is stated on this page and restored on return. For a measured mock the affected block follows the existing pause contract at 02 · 5.4 — it is not re-decided here.
One action only. No “discard”, no “start again”, no second warning. A learner cannot be asked to choose between their draft and signing in.
Return is exact. Back into the same attempt, same item, conditions row re-rendered first. Never Coach, never a summary of what was lost.
Recording is the one case where the notice waits: if capture is live, the recording is finalised and queued by UploadStatus before the interstitial replaces the shell, so a spoken turn is never dropped by a token expiry.
Copy rules for all five states. Three sentences maximum, 56 ch measure, no first person, no apology theatre. Faults on our side open with the existing “This wasn't you.” string; an ordinary sign-in or an expiry does not, because nothing failed. Never a provider name, never a technical code in the sentence — a support reference, if one is needed, sits in a mono line under the action and reads as a reference, not as a diagnosis.
U3 — Asynchronous capability status · one banner family for AI, STT and TTS

The banner stops promising a time it cannot keep.

01 · §2.11 has one pending banner and the string “Usually under a minute”. That string is retired: it promises a provider time the accepted policy forbids. The banner becomes a six-state contract on a measured policy, and the same six states serve Writing evaluation, Speaking evaluation, speech recognition and speech generation — a learner never learns which capability is which.

TIMING POLICY · WHAT THE LEARNER SEES, WHEN
0 – 15 s
Skeleton, no words
The shape of the result, static and unanimated (01 · §2.12). No spinner, no estimate, no banner.
15 – 30 s
Slow · still working
First useful feedback was expected by now. One amber line appears in place, and the learner may leave.
≥ 30 s
Explicit Pending
Evidence validity becomes pending, the attempt is released, and arrival is announced later. No indefinite wait state exists.
The thresholds are policy, not decoration: they are the only two numbers in this family, they are never shown to the learner as numbers, and no copy anywhere states an expected duration. Audio has the stricter 7-day ceiling from 01 · §3.6; when it is reached, the outcome is stated as unresolved rather than left pending forever.
1 · PROCESSING · 0–15 S
No copy at all. The result's own shape is the only signal. Nothing is announced.
2 · SLOW · 15–30 S

This is taking longer than usual. You can start Listening now — nothing else is waiting on it.
Amber, informational, in place. Validity is still valid; nothing has been decided yet.
3 · PENDING · ≥30 S

We'll tell you when it arrives. Your Readiness stays as it is until then, and the rest of today is unaffected.

4 · PARTIAL
Two of four content points checked
What you see below is complete. Structure and range are still being checked.
Validity partially_valid, and the unchecked scope is named. Never a number over a partial basis — the estimate waits.
5 · UNAVAILABLE + FALLBACK
This wasn't you.
Feedback is delayed on our side. Your answer is saved, and the objective marking below is complete — that part doesn't need us.
Neutral family, not amber: this is a fault, not a wait. The deterministic part of the result is shown; the judged part is withheld, never guessed.
6 · RETRY WITH A FRESH SAMPLE
This wasn't you.
We couldn't hear enough of your answer to judge it, so it doesn't count as evidence. One more turn on the same prompt is enough.
Validity not_counted. Stays visible in history so the record is complete, and never renders as a low result.
Which family, and why. States 2–4 are amber because the system is still working as designed and the learner is waiting. States 5–6 are the neutral grey dashed family because something failed on our side. That boundary is the whole point: amber means wait, grey means not your fault, red never appears in this family at all.
Invariants. One capability banner per screen, docked in the banner slot (U1). Never a modal, never a blocking overlay, never a spinner past 15 s. Never a provider name, model name, queue position, retry counter or percentage. A local capability failure never blocks unrelated coaching — the rest of the plan stays available in every one of these six states.
U4 — Save and transfer · two statuses, one grammar

Kept here is a different promise from sent to us.

AutosaveStatus already answers is my work safe on this device? It cannot answer has it reached you? — different owner, different failure, different recovery. That is the distinct responsibility that earns one new primitive, UploadStatus, shared by Speaking capture, Writing submission and mock submission. Both are mono status lines in the grammar of 01 · §2.12: never a modal, never blocking, never a toast that carries the only copy of the message.

U5 — Neutral fault ownership and scoped recovery

Every system fault owes the learner a scope, not just an apology.

The three-family colour boundary in 01 · P5 is intact. The delta is structural: SystemFailureBanner gains a required scope line, so a fault can never read as global. Architecture added four new fault sources — identity, transfer, capability, storage — and each of them is local by construction.

U7 — Focus, live regions, reduced motion

Every new state is testable the same way the old ones are.

The ten surfaces and four forbidden patterns in 04 · a11y are unchanged. Below is what those tests mean for the new states, plus the announcement strings — implement them verbatim, in the existing style: one fact, one consequence, one next action.

FOCUS
Interstitial replaces the shell. Focus moves to the notice heading — the first new element, never the top of the document. The heading is the page's h1; the mark is not focusable.
It is a page, not a dialog. No focus trap, no Esc, no inert background — there is no background. Tab order is heading → body → primary → secondary, and nothing else exists.
Return restores position. After sign-in the learner lands with focus on the first element of the region they left: the conditions row of the attempt, or the destination's page title.
Status lines never steal focus. Capability banners and UploadStatus appear without moving focus. When a failure adds an action, that action is reachable by Tab from the banner text and is a 44×44 target.
LIVE REGIONS
Polite, with two exceptions kept and one added. Final-5-minutes and recording failure stay assertive. Sign-out during a timed attempt becomes the third — the learner is producing evidence against a clock and cannot be told late.
Progress is not announced. Upload percentage updates in a region marked off from live announcement, exactly as timer ticks are. Only entering and leaving a state is announced.
One announcement per transition. Slow → Pending announces once, on arrival at Pending. Retry loops announce nothing until they stop.
Status has a word, always. No new state is carried by colour, a dot or a track alone; the mono label is the accessible name and it is visible, not sr-only.

EFFECT ON PHASE 3
F1 and F5 shrink to composition. Identity and capture families now assemble SessionNotice, UploadStatus and the bare shell instead of inventing them. F1 draws no new component at all.
F2 loses a primitive. Export delivery and purge reuse the UploadStatus state set with different nouns; what remains is copy plus owner decisions D6 and D7.
F4, F3, F7, F8 are untouched. They were never foundation. Their scope, and the eight-family cap, stand exactly as the Phase 1 audit set them.

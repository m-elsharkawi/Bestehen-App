00 — Brief and constraints

A logo system for the threshold between learning and passing.

Six concept territories explored in black and white, screened against eleven criteria, three refined into choice-ready finalists with labelled variation axes. One direction is recommended; none of the alternatives has been deleted.

Brand promise
Train for the exam. Know when you're ready.
Personality
Calm, focused, diagnostic, direct, evidence-based.
Architecture
Bestehen dominant. / App and / Corpus subordinate descriptors.
Hard constraint
No TELC resemblance. No accreditation seal. No pass guarantee.
Must feel

Calm · focused · modern · intelligent · precise · practical · warm enough for daily study · serious enough to support a readiness decision · distinctive at 16 px.

Ruled out before drawing

Graduation cap · open book · speech bubble · tick in a circle · trophy, medal, star, rocket, bullseye, upward arrow · flag, map, black-red-gold · brain, robot, sparkles, node clouds · shield or seal · a plain B in a rounded square.

Design criteria fixed before drawing

Every territory had to survive four gates in this order: (1) readable as pure black-and-white geometry, (2) legible at 16 px, (3) able to carry / App and / Corpus without a second mark, (4) conceptually specific to exam readiness rather than education in general. Colour, gradient and motion were withheld until after the gates.

01 — Exploration · six concept territories

Six different construction logics, not six colourways.

Each territory below is drawn in one colour first. The small-size strip shows the same geometry at 32, 24 and 16 px with no optical corrections applied — deliberately, so that fragility shows.

T1

Pass Line

Evidence rising until it crosses the line that actually matters.

Construction
Four measured bars on a shared baseline, increasing in height; one horizontal rule set at pass height and drawn wider than the bars so it reads as an external standard, not a container.
Brand link
The rule is the pass range; the bars are accumulating evidence. Two bars sit under it — the mark admits that readiness is not finished.
Distinctive
The overhanging rule. Almost every progress-bar mark encloses its bars; this one is measured against something outside itself.
Risk
Bar charts are common in analytics branding. Needs the overhang and the below-line bar to stay away from generic dashboard iconography.

T2

Threshold B

The letter B rebuilt so that its crossbar is the pass line, continuing past the letter on both sides.

Construction
Monoline B: one stem, two arc bowls of equal depth meeting at the vertical midpoint. The crossbar is not drawn as part of the letter — a single full-width rule is laid through the letter at exactly that midpoint.
Brand link
Bestehen and the threshold are one shape. The letter cannot be completed without the line: the brand is only itself once the standard is met.
Distinctive
The line is a shared part, borrowed by the letter. It leaves the counter shapes open, which is the opposite of the sealed, enclosed monogram badge.
Risk
A monogram must fight the "letter in a box" cliché. Solved by refusing the box and letting the rule bleed past the letter.

06 — Recommended system · FINALIZED ✓

Finalized: Concept B, variant B1 — Threshold B.

B1 is the only candidate that is simultaneously a monogram, a concept and a favicon. The rule gives Bestehen a structural device that scales across the whole system — it becomes the crossbar in the symbol, the divider before / App and / Corpus, the pass marker in readiness graphics, and the rule under a document header — without ever needing colour to be understood.

A is the stronger storyteller but skews analytics; C is the most original but too cool to lead a learner product. Both remain fully specified above and can be promoted without restarting the work.

07 — Brand architecture

One brand, two descriptors, one rule of subordination.

Bestehen is set at 100% weight and size. The slash sits at 0.72× the wordmark size, the descriptor at 0.72× size and one weight lighter. That single ratio is the whole system.

SLASH TREATMENT

Two approved settings, no others. Neutral — slash in ink at 55% opacity, for Corpus and all document use. Structural — slash in Bestehen Green, for App and marketing. The slash never takes the descriptor's weight and never grows to match Bestehen. This is what keeps the naming from reading as a code path.

WORDMARK CONSTRUCTION

Base typeface Instrument Sans SemiBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1 — permits modification and embedding; verify the current release before production). Modifications for the master: tracking −0.03 em, st pair kerned −6 units, terminals cut horizontally, and the t crossbar shortened to the rule's weight. The delivered master must be outlined; the live-type version is for development only.

09 — Usage guidelines

Mini brand guide.

CLEAR SPACE · UNIT = STROKE WIDTH
Bestehen

Clear space on all four sides = 2× stroke width (2 units of the symbol's 11-unit stroke, i.e. 18% of symbol height). Measured from the rule's ends and the wordmark's cap height, not from the letter's bowls.

ALIGNMENT WITH TEXT

In headers, align the symbol's rule to the wordmark's optical centre — not to the baseline. Symbol height = 1.15× the wordmark's cap height. Gap between symbol and wordmark = 0.34× symbol height; between wordmark and slash = 0.16×.

MOTION PRINCIPLES

Only one motion is sanctioned: the rule drawing left→right, 240 ms, ease-out, once. Never loops, never bounces, never celebrates. The mark is complete and recognisable in its static state, and reduced-motion users see only that.

EXAM-TARGET INDEPENDENCE

Bestehen never appears adjacent to, above, or in a shared frame with a TELC or other provider mark. Exam names appear as plain text in the product's own type, never as a logo. Nothing in the identity may be described as official, certified or approved. The mark stays valid if the target exam changes.

PROHIBITED USES
Never stretch, condense or change proportions.
Never recolour outside the approved colourways. Risk Red is a status colour, never a logo colour.
Never add shadow, outline, bevel or gradient to the master.
Never rotate the mark or detach the rule from the letter's midpoint.
BesteApp
Never enlarge / App or / Corpus above the umbrella name, and never change Bestehen's spelling or capitalisation.
Never place on busy or low-contrast surfaces without a solid protection field.
100%
Never use the symbol as a pass guarantee, certification seal or score badge.
ORIGINALITY AND SIMILARITY REVIEW

Category territories checked conceptually: mascot-led language learning (a character or animal head — not used here); syllable/speech-bubble marks; abstract globe and chat glyphs; certification seals and laurel/shield devices; tick-in-circle completion marks; node-and-sparkle AI marks. None of those constructions appears in B1.

Residual risk: monoline letterform monograms are a crowded space in general. The mitigation is structural rather than stylistic — the crossbar is a separate, over-length element shared with the wordmark and product graphics, which is what makes the construction specific rather than a stylised letter.

No trademark clearance is claimed. This is a design-level collision review only. A professional trademark search and legal clearance in the relevant classes and jurisdictions remains a separate, required step before public commercial use.

ACCESSIBILITY AND PRODUCTION CHECKS
✓  Ink on Warm Paper 14.4:1; Paper on Ink 14.4:1 (both AAA).
✓  Bestehen Green on Warm Paper 3.1:1 — passes the 3:1 non-text graphic threshold. For green text use Deep Green (5.6:1).
✓  Green Light #3FB484 on Deep Ink 6.1:1 for reversed use.
✓  Recognisable with colour removed: the construction is line-and-counter, not hue.
✓  No meaning carried by animation; the optional rule draw is decorative only.
✓  Flat geometry only — no raster effects, no gradient, no shadow in the master.
✓  Three paths plus one rule; no accidental overlaps or redundant anchors.
✓  Strokes are expanded to filled outlines in the production master so scaling cannot alter weight.
